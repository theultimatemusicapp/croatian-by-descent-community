## Sample only — no news event reported

This page demonstrates the business news format. No event, announcement, statistics or quotations have been invented.

## What a finished story will include

The reporter must verify the event against a primary source, write an original account and include the source name and direct link, publication date, author and a relevant licensed photograph. The source link below is an editorial starting point, not evidence of an event.

## Context and follow-up

A finished report should explain what is known, what remains uncertain and why the story matters. Claims needing a reply or additional verification must be resolved before publication. Corrections should be recorded transparently.
