## Begin with a normal week

A useful Zadar visit should include ordinary errands as well as the waterfront. List what needs to be close to home: shops, work, healthcare, schools if relevant and activities you would use regularly. Test the routes at the times you would actually travel.

## Compare areas through daily routines

Walk through potential neighbourhoods at different times. Note noise, shade, parking, stair access and the distance to the services you need. A view cannot tell you how a home feels in winter or how convenient it is after dark.

## Housing and costs

Request dated, written offers for the tenancy period you need. Ask which utilities are included, whether the lease covers the full year and who deals with maintenance. This guide intentionally does not publish current rental prices without a dated source.

## Transport and work

Check current routes and timetables with the local operator. If you work remotely, test connectivity at the actual property and consider where you would work during a disruption. Research work opportunities in your field rather than assuming a visitor economy will provide the role you want.

## Nature, lifestyle and community

Think about how access to the coast fits your routine, including weather and travel time. Look for recurring activities and opportunities to practise Croatian. Ask residents about both the benefits and inconveniences of the area they know.
