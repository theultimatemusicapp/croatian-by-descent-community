## Map the real journey

Check how you reach the stop, where you change and how you get home after an evening activity. Include walking time and the last return service.

## Verify before travelling

Use the operating company’s current timetable for your travel date. Check service-day notes, seasonal changes, ticket conditions and luggage arrangements. Save an offline copy of essential details.

## Test your regular routes

Try the commute or shopping journey at the relevant time. If you will depend on a connection, plan what happens when it is missed. Compare the whole journey rather than only the advertised ride time.

## Put this into your moving plan

Save your findings with dates and sources. Recheck anything time-sensitive before making a commitment. Continue through the Moving to Croatia hub for related planning guides.
