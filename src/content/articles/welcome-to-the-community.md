## A shared starting point

Croatia means different things to different people. It might be the place your grandparents talked about, a country you are considering moving to, or simply somewhere you want to understand beyond a short visit.

Croatian By Descent Community brings those starting points together. The platform is created by Carl Tomich, an Australian-born creator with Croatian heritage who recently became a Croatian citizen. He is documenting life, ancestry, relocation and his experiences in Croatia.

## What you will find here

The community is being built around practical questions: where to live, how to prepare for a move, how to research family records and what everyday life feels like. Alongside the guides, we want to make room for personal stories and conversations with diaspora members and returnees.

The first guides on this site are clearly marked samples for editorial review. We will not present invented news, interviews, quotes or statistics as fact. Videos are selected individually before they appear in the library.

## Bring your own perspective

There is room for both enthusiasm and difficulty. Finding a connection does not remove the work of learning a language, building friendships or navigating unfamiliar systems. Stories about the challenges belong here too.

Explore the moving and citizenship sections through the main navigation, meet Carl on the About page, or use Contact & submissions to propose a story when the submission service opens.
