## Before adding a video

This sample contains no video. A YouTube URL must first be added to the approved video list with Carl’s approval. The build rejects article embeds that are not on that list.

## Written context

Introduce the actual subject and location of the approved recording. Describe what the viewer will learn without inventing events or implying that illustrative footage establishes a factual claim.

## Key moments

After watching the recording, add accurate timestamps and a concise written account. Link to official sources for any legal, immigration, tax or property statement.

## Continue exploring

Add related article slugs and connect the video to its relevant city, region and subject hub. Use a descriptive title and an original summary so the written page remains useful on its own.
