## Ask the bank directly

Request a current list of documents and eligibility conditions for the account you need. Compare account fees, cards, cash access, online banking and customer support in a language you understand.

## Compare the full transfer cost

Look at the amount received after fees and exchange-rate margins, not only the advertised transfer charge. Confirm limits and timing before a deposit or other time-sensitive payment.

## Keep a backup

Retain a safe alternative way to access funds while an account is being opened or a transfer is delayed. Keep records that explain the origin of larger transfers and ask the provider what it requires.
