## Define a good fit

Write down your non-negotiables: income, access to services, language support, climate, family contact and the kind of community you want. Rank them before looking at attractive property listings.

## Try the ordinary version

During a trial stay, work your usual hours, shop for everyday groceries and follow a realistic budget. Include a quieter period if possible so you do not judge the move only by a holiday.

## Make a decision you can revisit

Agree on a review point and keep a realistic exit reserve. A move can be worthwhile without needing to be permanent. Talk through the plan with everyone whose daily life it will change.

Save your findings with dates and sources. Recheck anything time-sensitive before making a commitment. Continue through the Moving to Croatia hub for related planning guides.
