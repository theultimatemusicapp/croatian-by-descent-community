## Expect a learning period

Unfamiliar systems can make simple tasks take more effort. Keep a record of instructions and appointments, and allow extra time for documents or follow-up questions.

## Be honest about support

Think about how you will cope with loneliness, a disrupted routine or family far away. Discuss practical help and regular contact before the move rather than waiting for a difficult week.

## Define your fallback

Keep funds and documents accessible, and decide when to review your plan. Changing a neighbourhood, adjusting the timeline or returning elsewhere can be sensible choices rather than failures.

Save your findings with dates and sources. Recheck anything time-sensitive before making a commitment. Continue through the Moving to Croatia hub for related planning guides.
