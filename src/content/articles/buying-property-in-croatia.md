## Set the brief before the search

Decide whether you need a permanent home, a renovation project or occasional use. List access, maintenance, services and realistic travel needs alongside your budget.

## Keep advice independent

Ask a qualified independent lawyer to examine the ownership record, encumbrances, boundaries, permissions and your ability to acquire the specific property. A listing or an agent’s assurance is not a substitute for document checks.

## Budget for the full project

Request dated estimates for professional services, transaction costs and any necessary work. Inspect the building with an appropriate professional. Do not rely on headline asking prices as evidence of what a purchase will ultimately cost.
