## Choose useful first situations

Start with introductions, appointments, shopping and questions you ask repeatedly. Keep a small notebook of expressions you have actually needed rather than collecting long lists you never use.

## Create a repeatable practice

Choose a lesson or study period you can maintain. Add short listening and speaking practice between lessons. Ask a tutor to help with situations you expect to encounter.

## Leave room for mistakes

Tell people you are learning and ask whether they have time to practise. Keep important legal or medical conversations supported by competent interpretation when needed; everyday practice is a different task.

Save your findings with dates and sources. Recheck anything time-sensitive before making a commitment. Continue through the Moving to Croatia hub for related planning guides.
