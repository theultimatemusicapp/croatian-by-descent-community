## Clarify permission and obligations

Ask the competent authority which residence and work arrangements fit your situation. Discuss the proposed location with your employer and obtain advice on tax, social insurance and business obligations before working from Croatia.

## Test the workspace

Check upload speed, call quality, desk ergonomics, noise and a backup connection at the actual property. A general coverage map is not a test of your room.

## Protect your routine

Map working hours against your clients’ time zones. Plan breaks and social contact, and check access to a suitable alternative workspace before you need it. Do not assume a digital-nomad label resolves every legal question.
