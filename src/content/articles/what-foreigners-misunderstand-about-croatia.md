## Separate a visit from a life

A holiday concentrates memorable moments. Living somewhere includes administration, chores and ordinary tired days. Compare like with like when evaluating your experience.

## Avoid broad conclusions

One frustrating appointment or generous conversation does not describe an entire country. Ask what happened in that specific situation and seek more than one perspective.

## Ask instead of assuming

Check the practical meaning of an instruction, opening time or agreement. Learning the language and asking respectful questions can reveal context that a quick impression misses.
