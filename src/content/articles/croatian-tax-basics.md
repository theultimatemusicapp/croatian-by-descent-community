## Make a complete picture

List where you live and work, your travel dates, family and housing connections, income sources, investments and businesses. Include foreign accounts and expected changes without guessing their tax treatment.

## Ask about each obligation

Discuss residence, income, reporting, social contributions and any relevant treaty separately. A citizenship document or a commonly repeated day count should not be treated as a complete tax analysis.

## Keep advice and evidence

Ask the adviser to document assumptions, deadlines and what would change the conclusion. Keep records of work locations and income, and use the Croatian Tax Administration for current official information.

## Put this into your moving plan

Save your findings with dates and sources. Recheck anything time-sensitive before making a commitment. Continue through the Moving to Croatia hub for related planning guides.
