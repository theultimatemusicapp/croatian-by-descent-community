## Start with the role

List employers and sectors where your actual skills apply. Compare job descriptions and note language requirements, qualifications and whether your experience transfers to the role.

## Understand the offer

Ask for written information on duties, working hours, pay, contract duration and the employing entity. Seek clarification before accepting terms you cannot read or understand.

## Verify the right to work

Confirm work authorisation requirements for your nationality and circumstances with the relevant authority. Use the Croatian Employment Service as a starting point and verify specific vacancies with the employer.

## Put this into your moving plan

Save your findings with dates and sources. Recheck anything time-sensitive before making a commitment. Continue through the Moving to Croatia hub for related planning guides.
