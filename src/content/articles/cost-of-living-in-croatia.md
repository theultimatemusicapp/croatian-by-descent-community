## Create three budgets

Separate arrival costs, a normal month and an expensive month. Include deposits, document services, travel, equipment and emergency funds alongside rent, bills and groceries.

## Gather comparable evidence

Save the date and location of every quote. Compare properties for the same lease duration, size and included utilities. Check whether an attractive figure is a nightly holiday rate or a long-term offer.

## Test your assumptions

Track an ordinary week of spending on a scouting visit. Record what you would buy regularly and what was a holiday expense. Revisit your budget when your income, household or location changes.

## Put this into your moving plan

Save your findings with dates and sources. Recheck anything time-sensitive before making a commitment. Continue through the Moving to Croatia hub for related planning guides.
