## Introduction — to be completed after the interview

This is an editorial template, not an interview with a real person. No participant, biography, quotes or answers have been invented.

## The conversation

Suggested questions for a future participant:

- What connects your family to Croatia?
- Where did you move from, and what made you consider the move?
- What was different from your expectations?
- What helped you build an everyday routine?
- What challenges would you want another person to understand?
- What would you do differently if starting again?

## Written summary

After the conversation, describe the participant’s experience in context. Distinguish their individual circumstances from general guidance. Add a link to an approved recording and related guides.

## Key quotes

Add only accurately transcribed, approved quotations with enough surrounding context to preserve their meaning. This sample intentionally contains no quotes.

## Full article

Expand the approved conversation into an original article. Confirm names, locations, dates, image permissions and any requested privacy boundaries before publication.
