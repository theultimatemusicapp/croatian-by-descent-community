## Confirm your coverage

Ask the relevant health insurer or authority what cover applies to your status and when it begins. Request written clarification of any gap between existing and new arrangements.

## Plan continuity

If you use regular treatment, ask your current clinician how to prepare a medical summary and an appropriate supply for travel. Confirm local access to ongoing care before the move.

## Make a local information list

Identify suitable primary care, pharmacies and out-of-hours services. Confirm arrangements directly with providers, including language availability, payment and appointment procedures. This guide does not advise on treatment.

## Put this into your moving plan

Save your findings with dates and sources. Recheck anything time-sensitive before making a commitment. Continue through the Moving to Croatia hub for related planning guides.
