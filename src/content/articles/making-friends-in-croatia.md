## Show up more than once

Choose a class, sport, volunteer role or interest group you would enjoy for its own sake. Repeated contact gives conversation a chance to become familiarity.

## Make small invitations

Suggest a coffee or a walk with a clear time and place. Be considerate of people’s work and family commitments, and avoid reading too much into a single declined invitation.

## Build more than one circle

Keep existing friendships while making room for local ones. Diaspora and newcomer groups can be helpful starting points, but a shared interest may connect you to people beyond the moving experience.
