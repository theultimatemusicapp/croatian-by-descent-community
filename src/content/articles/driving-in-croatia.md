## Check your documents

Ask the competent authority how your driving licence is treated for your status and intended stay. Verify vehicle registration and insurance requirements if you plan to bring a car.

## Research the places you will drive

Review parking options at home and work, road access and seasonal conditions. Ask about the dimensions and access of a parking space before assuming it suits your vehicle.

## Keep current official information

Use the Croatian Automobile Club for travel information and the Ministry of the Interior for the rules relevant to your documents. Do not rely on a guide to replace current road signs or official instructions.

## Put this into your moving plan

Save your findings with dates and sources. Recheck anything time-sensitive before making a commitment. Continue through the Moving to Croatia hub for related planning guides.
