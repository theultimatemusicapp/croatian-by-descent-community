## Ask about the whole tenancy

Confirm start and end dates, the notice procedure, deposit terms and whether the property is available throughout your intended stay. Get important assurances in writing.

## Inspect what affects daily life

Check heating and cooling, damp, window condition, internet, water pressure, noise and access. Ask who pays which bills and who arranges repairs. Photograph the agreed inventory and condition with the owner’s permission.

## Verify before paying

Confirm the identity and authority of the person offering the home. Read the agreement in a language you understand and seek independent advice on uncertain terms. Do not send a deposit just because someone creates urgency.

Save your findings with dates and sources. Recheck anything time-sensitive before making a commitment. Continue through the Moving to Croatia hub for related planning guides.
