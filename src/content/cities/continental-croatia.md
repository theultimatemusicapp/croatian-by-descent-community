Continental Croatia is used here as a broad editorial guide to inland bases, including places around Varaždin, Karlovac, and Osijek and the wider northern and eastern countryside. It is not presented as a current statistical region, one municipality, or one property market. Zagreb has a separate city guide, and Gorski kotar is discussed in the Kvarner guide. The purpose is to help readers look beyond a coast-versus-capital choice while remaining precise about where daily life would happen.

Start with a named town and the household's essential journeys. A central Osijek apartment, a Varaždin home, and a rural property outside Karlovac offer different routines, even if all are described as inland. This guide uses official destination, transport, park, employment, and property sources for orientation. Dated price evidence appears in the tables, with the source, location and conditions attached to each figure.
