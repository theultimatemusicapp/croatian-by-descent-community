A move to Dubrovnik is a decision about a compact but varied place, not simply about living inside the famous walls. Compare the historic centre, nearby slopes, Lapad, Gruž and other candidate areas through the journeys and rooms you will actually use. An extraordinary view can be part of a good home, but it cannot answer questions about steps, storage, commuting or an uninterrupted lease.

This guide treats Dubrovnik as a place for ordinary life across the year. Dated price evidence appears in the tables, with the source, location and conditions attached to each figure. Use its comparisons to gather dated local evidence and to distinguish a short visit from the household you would establish. A trial that includes work, shopping and quiet evenings is more informative than a visit built entirely around the city's best-known sights.
