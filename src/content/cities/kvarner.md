For this guide, Kvarner means a regional choice encompassing Rijeka and its surroundings, the Opatija and Crikvenica–Vinodol coastal areas, the islands of Krk, Cres, Lošinj, and Rab, and the Gorski kotar hinterland. It is an editorial planning scope, not a claim that the region is one municipality, housing market, or commuting area. Rijeka has its own city guide; this page helps compare different types of base across the wider region.

A household should first choose between an urban, mainland coastal, island, or inland arrangement, then investigate an actual settlement. Official regional tourism material separates coastal, island, and Gorski kotar experiences for good reason: these lead to different practical questions. Dated price evidence appears in the tables, with the source, location and conditions attached to each figure. Its purpose is to make those questions concrete before a familiar holiday destination becomes a long-term housing commitment.
