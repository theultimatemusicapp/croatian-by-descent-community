Split is a useful place to test whether your imagined return to the Adriatic works as an ordinary urban life. The harbour, historic centre and beaches form only part of the decision. You also need a comfortable home, a manageable route to work and a way to build relationships outside a visitor's schedule. Treat a scouting stay as a rehearsal for those routines.

Compare specific streets and buildings rather than choosing between a postcard version of the centre and an equally simplified idea of the suburbs. A household working from home, a hospital employee and a retired couple may reasonably choose different parts of the city. The sections below help make those choices explicit. They contain planning guidance, not a live survey of rental prices, property values or current vacancies.
