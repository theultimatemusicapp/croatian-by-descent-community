Dalmatia is used here as a broad regional planning label for the Adriatic area around Zadar, Šibenik, Split and Dubrovnik, their associated islands and relevant inland communities. It is not treated as one municipality, one county or one uniform housing market. The practical boundary for a move is the set of places whose services, work and transport you can realistically use from your chosen home.

Start by choosing a type of life before choosing a famous destination: a larger coastal city, a smaller mainland town, an island settlement or an inland base. Then narrow the search to actual addresses and service routes. The guidance below contains no regional live-price survey or claim that opportunities are evenly distributed. Its purpose is to help you compare distinct parts of Dalmatia without letting a shared coastal identity conceal important differences.
