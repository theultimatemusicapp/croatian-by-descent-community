This guide covers the Croatian part of Istria, with practical comparisons between towns and inland settlements rather than a single regional lifestyle. Pula, Rovinj, Poreč, Pazin, Labin and a small village do not offer identical housing, transport or work arrangements. Nearby places across national borders require their own research and are not included as though they were Croatian neighbourhoods. Use the eight sections to choose a specific base and test its everyday implications. The guide supplies no live rental-price dataset, investment forecast or current vacancy list.
