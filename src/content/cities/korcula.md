Korčula means both an island and the town at its eastern end. This guide covers the island while identifying the town separately; Vela Luka, Blato, Lumbarda and smaller settlements should not be read as interchangeable neighbourhoods. Choose your base before evaluating island life, because mainland access, work and ordinary errands depend on that choice. The following sections offer practical ways to assess a real stay.
