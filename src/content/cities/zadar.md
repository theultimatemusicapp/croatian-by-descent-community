Zadar is a city guide rather than a guide to every island and resort in Zadar County. Start with the address from which you would live: the peninsula, a residential street farther inland and a coastal location such as Diklo produce different routines. The eight sections below help connect the appeal of the waterfront with housing, work and journeys you can actually sustain. They contain no live rental-price dataset or claim that a quoted holiday experience represents a resident’s budget. Named attractions and transport resources provide local reference points; the comparisons are practical exercises for your own circumstances.
